# MDS RD Project Builder — Customer Policy

**Effective date:** 20 September 2026  
**Operator:** Magen Daniel Systems LLP (“MDS”)  
**Application:** MDS RD Project Builder

This policy applies to customers using MDS RD Project Builder to request, review, approve, fund, and manage engineering or R&D work through MDS.

## 1. Role of MDS
MDS provides professional R&D and engineering services and manages project coordination through the application. Quotations, technical commitments, schedules, BOMs, and engineering deliverables become official only when approved by authorized MDS personnel.

AI-generated estimates, technical suggestions, BOMs, summaries, or recommendations are preliminary until reviewed and approved by an MDS engineer or authorized reviewer.

## 2. Customer information and project scope
Customers must provide complete and accurate project requirements, files, constraints, and technical information. MDS may request clarification before work begins or while a project is in progress.

Clarifications needed to complete an already agreed scope do not by themselves create a new charge. If the customer requests a material scope change, additional deliverables, or work outside the approved scope, MDS may issue a revised quotation or milestone before the additional work starts.

## 3. Engineer proposals and service fee
For each engineer proposal delivered through MDS, a flat **USD 2.50 service fee** may be charged to the customer. This fee is separate from the engineer's compensation and is not deducted from the engineer's agreed project amount.

## 4. Payments and milestones
Projects may be divided into milestones. Work begins only after the relevant payment or milestone is confirmed.

- PayPal payments may be confirmed automatically only after server-side verification of the payment status, amount, and currency.
- Bank transfer, UPI, company QR, and other manual payment methods are confirmed only after verification by MDS finance or authorized staff.
- A milestone marked as paid does not by itself mean the work has been accepted or completed.

## 5. Deliverables and approvals
Customers must review submitted work within a reasonable period and provide clear approval, requested corrections, or documented issues. MDS may require engineering review before a milestone is marked complete.

## 6. Confidentiality and files
Project files, engineering documents, customer materials, technical data, and communications exchanged through MDS are confidential unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

Customers must not disclose MDS confidential material or engineer-confidential material to third parties without authorization.

## 7. Commercial communication
Commercial discussions, payment instructions, milestone approvals, and project-file exchanges should remain within authorized MDS channels. Customers must not attempt to bypass MDS for payment, contracting, or project administration where MDS is the contracting or coordinating party.

## 8. Conduct
Fraud, abuse, impersonation, misleading project information, misuse of the platform, threats, or attempts to manipulate payments or engineering records may result in project suspension or account restriction.

## 9. Cancellations and refunds
Cancellations and refunds are governed by the separate **MDS RD Project Builder — Customer Cancellation & Refund Policy**. Refund eligibility depends on payment status, milestone status, work already completed, accepted deliverables, and applicable law.

## 10. Mandatory law
Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable consumer, payment, or other mandatory law.
