# MDS RD Project Builder — Confidentiality & Intellectual Property Policy

**Effective date:** 20 September 2026  
**Operator:** Magen Daniel Systems LLP (“MDS”)  
**Application:** MDS RD Project Builder

## 1. Confidential project information
Project specifications, schematics, PCB files, Gerbers, firmware, source code, BOMs, CAD files, prototypes, test data, business information, credentials, customer materials, and internal MDS documentation may be confidential.

## 2. Access limitation
Users must access and use confidential information only for the authorized project purpose.

## 3. No unauthorized disclosure
Confidential project information must not be published, forwarded, copied to third parties, uploaded to public repositories, or reused for unrelated work without written authorization.

## 4. Engineer obligations
Engineers and technical service providers must comply with applicable NDAs and project-specific IP terms. They must not reuse confidential MDS or customer designs as portfolio material, training material, public examples, or third-party deliverables without authorization.

## 5. Customer obligations
Customers must protect confidential MDS technical information, quotations, internal engineering material, and engineer-confidential work product provided through the project.

## 6. Intellectual property ownership
Ownership and licensing of project deliverables are determined by the applicable quotation, statement of work, NDA, IP assignment, license, or other written agreement. This policy does not replace those agreements.

## 7. Open-source and third-party material
Where open-source or third-party material is used, applicable license obligations must be identified and respected. Open-source material does not become proprietary merely because it is used in an MDS project.

## 8. Security incidents
Suspected unauthorized access, disclosure, credential exposure, or loss of confidential project material must be reported to MDS promptly.
