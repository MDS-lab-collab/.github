# MDS RD Project Builder — Payment, Milestone & Dispute Policy

**Effective date:** 20 September 2026  
**Operator:** Magen Daniel Systems LLP (“MDS”)  
**Application:** MDS RD Project Builder

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines how project payments, milestone states, payment verification, and commercial disputes are handled in MDS RD Project Builder.

## 2. Payment verification
A payment is treated as confirmed only after MDS or the application verifies the relevant transaction.

For PayPal, automated confirmation must rely on verified server-side payment status and matching transaction amount/currency. A browser return page alone is not sufficient proof of payment.

Manual payment methods such as bank transfer, UPI, or company QR require MDS finance verification.

## 3. Milestone states
A typical milestone may move through states such as:
- proposed;
- approved;
- awaiting payment;
- funded/paid;
- in progress;
- submitted for review;
- correction requested;
- approved/completed;
- cancelled; or
- disputed.

## 4. Technical acceptance is separate from payment
Payment confirmation authorizes work to proceed but does not automatically mean the deliverable is technically accepted.

## 5. Scope changes
Material scope changes should be documented before additional work begins. Normal clarification of the existing scope is not automatically a scope change.

## 6. Dispute review
Where a dispute occurs, MDS may review:
- project scope and quotation;
- milestone definition;
- payment records;
- customer and engineer communications;
- source files and revisions;
- technical review findings;
- delivery and acceptance history; and
- third-party payment status.

## 7. Fraud and payment anomalies
MDS may pause a project or payment where there is suspected fraud, duplicate payment, amount/currency mismatch, chargeback, unauthorized payment, or inconsistent transaction data.

## 8. Audit trail
Material payment and milestone actions should be recorded in the application audit trail where supported by the system.

## 9. Relationship to other policies
Customer refunds are governed by the Customer Cancellation & Refund Policy. Engineer compensation and reversals are governed by the Engineer Payment, Cancellation & Reversal Policy.
