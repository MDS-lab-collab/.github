# MDS RD Project Builder — Customer Cancellation & Refund Policy

**Effective date:** 20 September 2026  
**Operator:** Magen Daniel Systems LLP (“MDS”)  
**Application:** MDS RD Project Builder

This policy governs customer cancellations and refund requests for projects, milestones, and proposal-related charges processed through MDS RD Project Builder.

## 1. General principle
Refunds are based on the amount actually paid, the status of the relevant milestone, whether the engineer has formally accepted and started the work, work already performed, deliverables already provided or accepted, third-party costs already incurred, and applicable mandatory law.

## 2. Cancellation window before engineer acceptance
A customer may request cancellation during the cancellation window displayed for the relevant project or milestone, provided the engineer has not yet formally accepted and started the funded work.

The exact cancellation window may be defined in the project, quotation, milestone, or application record. If no specific period is displayed, MDS will use the documented project status and acceptance timestamp to determine whether work had started.

If cancellation is approved before engineer acceptance/work start, MDS may refund the unearned milestone amount.

Any non-recoverable payment-processor, currency-conversion, banking, or third-party charges may be handled according to the applicable payment method and mandatory law.

## 3. After engineer acceptance or work start
Once the engineer has formally accepted the milestone after the clarification stage and work has started, a customer may still request cancellation or a refund, but a full refund is not automatic.

MDS will review:
- work already completed;
- time and external costs already incurred;
- deliverables already submitted;
- whether the customer has accepted any part of the work;
- remaining unperformed scope;
- the engineer acceptance/start timestamp; and
- any documented non-conformity with the agreed scope.

Any approved refund will normally be limited to the unearned or unperformed portion of the relevant payment, subject to applicable law and written project terms.

## 4. Engineer responsibility after acceptance
Formal engineer acceptance after reasonable scope clarification means the engineer has accepted responsibility for performing the agreed work with reasonable professional care.

If delivered work materially fails the agreed scope, contains demonstrable engineering errors, or is materially incomplete, MDS may require correction and may take that non-conformity into account when deciding any refund or milestone payment.

This does not make the engineer responsible for later customer-requested scope changes, missing customer information that could not reasonably have been identified, or dependencies outside the engineer's control.

## 5. Completed or accepted milestones
A milestone that has been completed and accepted is generally not refundable solely because the customer later changes direction, no longer needs the work, or requests a different scope.

This does not remove any rights the customer may have for proven non-delivery, material non-conformity, fraud, duplicate payment, or rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## 6. Proposal service fee
The USD 2.50 engineer-proposal service fee is generally non-refundable after the proposal has been delivered to the customer, except in cases such as duplicate charging, technical billing error, or where required by applicable law.

## 7. PayPal refunds
Approved PayPal refunds should be returned through the original payment channel where practicable. PayPal disputes, reversals, chargebacks, or payment holds may pause project processing while MDS verifies the transaction.

## 8. Bank transfer, UPI, and QR refunds
Manual-payment refunds require verification by MDS finance. The customer may be asked to confirm the original payment reference and the destination account details required for a lawful refund.

## 9. Duplicate or incorrect payment
Verified duplicate payments or confirmed payment-processing errors are eligible for correction or refund.

## 10. Customer-requested scope changes
If the customer changes the project scope after work starts, previously completed work remains chargeable. New or changed work may require a new quotation or milestone.

## 11. How to request a refund
The customer should submit the request through an authorized MDS channel and include:
- project or milestone reference;
- payment reference;
- reason for the request;
- disputed deliverables, if any; and
- supporting evidence.

## 12. Review and decision
MDS will review the project record, milestone status, acceptance/start timestamp, engineering evidence, payment record, and applicable written agreement before deciding the refundable amount.

## 13. Mandatory law
Where applicable law grants the customer non-waivable cancellation, refund, or consumer rights, those rights prevail over any inconsistent term in this policy.
