# MDS RD Project Builder — Engineer Policy

**Effective date:** 20 September 2026  
**Operator:** Magen Daniel Systems LLP (“MDS”)  
**Application:** MDS RD Project Builder

This policy applies to engineers, freelancers, consultants, and technical service providers performing work through MDS RD Project Builder.

## 1. Engagement and scope
Engineers must perform only the scope approved by MDS for the relevant project or milestone.

Technical questions and clarifications required to complete the agreed scope are part of the normal engineering process and do not automatically change the agreed price.

If MDS requests a genuine material scope change, additional deliverables, or work outside the agreed scope, any price or schedule adjustment must be discussed and approved before the additional work begins.

## 2. Milestones
MDS may divide work into milestones. Engineers must complete the deliverables defined for the current milestone before requesting release or progression to the next milestone.

A funded milestone is not automatically accepted. MDS may perform technical, schematic, code, BOM, PCB, documentation, or other engineering review before approval.

## 3. Technical coordination
Engineers are expected to communicate clearly and raise technical uncertainties early. MDS may involve its internal engineers in technical decisions, component selection, architecture review, testing, and scope clarification.

The goal is coordinated development and technical alignment, not unilateral changes by either party.

## 4. Deliverables and evidence
Where applicable, engineers must provide source files, editable design files, BOMs, calculations, test results, logs, screenshots, design notes, and other agreed evidence.

Deliverables must be internally consistent and correspond to the correct project revision.

## 5. Pricing
The engineer's agreed compensation is not reduced by the separate customer proposal service fee charged by MDS.

An engineer may not increase the agreed project or milestone price solely because MDS asks for normal clarification, progress updates, or information necessary to complete the agreed scope.

## 6. Confidentiality and intellectual property
Engineers must protect MDS and customer confidential information and comply with any NDA, IP assignment, work-for-hire, licensing, or project-specific agreement that applies.

Confidential schematics, firmware, source code, product specifications, BOMs, credentials, customer files, and internal MDS information must not be reused, published, or shared except as authorized in writing.

## 7. No unauthorized subcontracting or external disclosure
Engineers must not transfer confidential project work to another person or subcontractor without prior written approval from MDS.

## 8. Commercial conduct
Engineers must not bypass MDS for payments, side agreements, or customer solicitation when the work was introduced or managed through MDS.

## 9. Quality and professional responsibility
Engineers must identify assumptions, limitations, unverified claims, and known risks. A design, schematic, BOM, codebase, or test result must not be represented as production-approved unless it has actually passed the required review and validation.

## 10. Cancellation, payment reversals, and compensation
Engineer payment, cancellation, reversal, and work-in-progress rules are governed by the separate **MDS RD Project Builder — Engineer Payment, Cancellation & Reversal Policy**.

## 11. Violations
Fraud, plagiarism, misrepresentation, unauthorized disclosure, deliberate delivery of false results, payment bypass, or serious professional misconduct may result in milestone rejection, project termination, account restriction, or other contractual remedies.
